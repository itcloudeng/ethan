Brief Plot Overview (2–3 points/sentences)
Ethan's original writing
The story begins with a young sniper posted in enemy territory. He is faced with many threats and soon encounters a highly skilled enemy sniper. After an intense duel which he prevails, he discovers the enemy sniper was his brother.
Thesis
Added to complete the outline
In “The Sniper,” Liam O'Flaherty uses characterization and situational irony to show how war can cause people to do things they normally wouldn't and later regret.

First Body Paragraph — Characterization

Topic Sentence
O'Flaherty uses characterization to show how the sniper changes because of the violence around him.

First Example

Context and Quote
At the beginning of the story, the sniper's eyes have the “cold gleam of the fanatic” and are the eyes of a man who is “used to looking at death.”
Analysis / Significance
This shows that the sniper is hardened and used to violence. War has made death seem like a normal part of his life.

Second Example

Context and Quote
After watching the enemy sniper die, “The lust of battle died in him. He became bitten by remorse.”
Analysis / Significance
His excitement about the fight disappears. He now feels guilty and horrified by what he has done.

Second Body Paragraph — Situational Irony

Topic Sentence
O'Flaherty also uses situational irony to show the terrible consequences of war.

First Example

Context and Quote
After the fight, the sniper becomes curious about the man he killed. He wonders if “he knew him” and if the man had been in his own company before the army split.
Analysis / Significance
This hints that the enemy may be someone familiar to him and prepares the reader for the surprise at the end.

Second Example

Context and Quote
When he reaches the body, “the sniper turned over the dead body and looked into his brother's face.”
Analysis / Significance
This is situational irony because the enemy he killed is actually his brother. The surprise shows how civil war can turn even family members against each other.

Conclusion — XYZ

eXplain — Revisit Thesis in a New Way
Through the sniper's change and the surprising ending, O'Flaherty shows what war can cause people to do.
whY — Why It Is Important
The sniper is used to violence at first, but after killing the enemy he begins to regret what he has done. Finding out that the enemy was his brother makes the consequences even worse.
Zinger — Takeaway Idea
Sometimes in war, people do not realize the line they have crossed until it is too late.
